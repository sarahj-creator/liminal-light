2. What information we collect

When you make a treatment request, we may receive your name, telephone/WhatsApp number, email address, parish/area, preferred date and time, selected treatment, preferred setting/style, and any optional notes you choose to send.

Please do not put detailed medical histories, diagnoses, medication lists, financial information, passwords or other highly sensitive information into the optional notes field. If information is needed to determine whether a treatment is appropriate, we will ask you for it through an appropriate channel.

We may also receive basic technical information needed to operate and secure the website, such as IP address, browser/device information and pages requested. Optional analytics is only enabled after your consent.

3. How we use personal data

  • To respond to enquiries and treatment requests.
  • To arrange, confirm and manage appointments.
  • To communicate with you about a requested service.
  • To maintain records required for accounting, legal or business purposes.
  • To protect the website, prevent abuse and maintain security.
  • Where you consent, to understand website usage through analytics.

4. Legal basis

Depending on the circumstances, processing may be necessary to respond to your request or take steps at your request before providing a service, to comply with legal obligations, for legitimate business and security interests, or because you have provided consent for a specific optional purpose.

5. Sharing

We may share relevant information with service providers who help us operate the website or provide requested services, such as hosting, communications, payment or analytics providers, where applicable. If you choose WhatsApp or email, your device will open the relevant application with your request pre-filled. Nothing is transmitted by the website until you send it through that application. WhatsApp and email are third-party services and their own privacy terms may apply.
