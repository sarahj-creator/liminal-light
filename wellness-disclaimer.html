1. General information

Liminal Light provides massage and wellness services intended to support relaxation, comfort and general wellbeing. Information on this website is educational and general in nature.

2. Not medical advice

Nothing on this website or in a Liminal Light massage session should be treated as medical advice, diagnosis or a substitute for professional healthcare. We do not diagnose or treat medical conditions.

3. Seek appropriate healthcare

If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, have recently had surgery or an injury, take medication, experience unexplained symptoms, or are unsure whether massage is appropriate for you, consult an appropriate healthcare professional before treatment.

4. Emergency situations

Liminal Light is not an emergency medical service. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact the appropriate emergency service or healthcare provider.

5. Individual results

Responses to massage and wellness practices vary between individuals. No particular physical, emotional or health outcome is guaranteed.
