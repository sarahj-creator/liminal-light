Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Our commitment

Liminal Light aims to make its website usable by as many people as reasonably possible. We work to improve keyboard navigation, readable text, colour contrast, clear labels and responsive design.

Known limitations

Some visual or third-party components may not provide a perfect experience for every assistive technology. We will review and improve accessibility as the website develops.

Need assistance?

If you have difficulty accessing information or requesting a service, contact info_liminallight@proton.me or call/WhatsApp +1 246 259 0091. We will try to provide reasonable assistance.
