Cookie Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files or similar technologies stored or accessed through your browser. Some are necessary for a website to work; others help a site understand usage or support optional features.

2. What Liminal Light currently uses

  • Necessary technologies: used for core website functionality and to remember your cookie preference.
  • Google Analytics: optional analytics used to understand aggregate website usage. It is not loaded until you choose "Accept analytics".

There are currently no social-media or video embeds built into the main page. Links to WhatsApp and email are user-initiated links rather than embedded tracking widgets.

3. Your choices

Analytics is optional. You can reject it, accept it, or change your choice using the "Cookie Settings" link in the website footer. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.

4. Third-party services

Google Analytics is provided by Google. Google may process technical information in accordance with its own terms and privacy documentation. The website also loads fonts from Google Fonts; this is a third-party resource request and is disclosed here so that visitors are aware of it.

5. Changes

We will update this policy if we add, remove or materially change cookies, analytics, advertising pixels, embeds or other tracking technologies.
